// THE_SUHLABS_GOVERNANCE_STACK: RESEARCH → FRAMEWORK → POLICY

Critical human institutions: aviation air traffic control, surgical pre-op checklists, and banking wire approvals: never permit the same actor to propose, authorize, execute, and grade their own actions. Autonomous artificial intelligence must follow that exact discipline.

1. EMPIRICAL RESEARCH
The Discovery of Failure

Proves that prompt-based guardrails fail. Models hack unasserted test suites, experience context degradation, and introduce orphan side effects.

2. THE DART FRAMEWORK
The Operating Loop

A deterministic 4-phase finite state machine (Declare, Authorize, Record, Triage) mathematically verified in Lean 4 to eliminate unmediated execution.

3. ENFORCEABLE POLICY
The Institutional Standard

Translates the loop into binding corporate CISO directives, FAR/DFARS procurement clauses, and NIST SP 800-207 Zero Trust compliance.

// THE_FOUR_RULES_OF_THE_DART_FRAMEWORK

OPERATING ENGINE
D · DECLARE
"No Secret Plans"

Ambient authority is prohibited. All intent, parameters, and side effects must be committed in frozen JSON schemas (Draft 2020-12) before execution.

A · AUTHORIZE
"No Self-Approvals"

Thinking an action is not permission. Planners never hold execution keys; an external Policy Enforcement Point (PEP) verifies quota and tokens.

R · RECORD
"No Phantom Actions"

If it is not written to the tamper-evident ledger before dispatch, it does not happen. Eliminates orphan side effects across crash recovery.

T · TRIAGE
"Never Grade Your Own Work"

Self-evaluation is void. Acceptance tests are mounted read-only (:ro) with negative controls, quarantining reward-hacked solutions automatically.

Agents Without Mechanical Gates

Autonomous coding agents suffer from semantic drift, reward hacking (passing tests that fail to assert), and prompt-only failures. Prompts are probabilistic suggestions; stability requires frozen schemas, read-only TDD test mounts, Git worktrees, and deny-by-default sandbox egress.
