MANDATORY CONTROLS

The DART Policy Framework: How to Keep AI Safe with Fixed Rules and Zero Trust

POLICY-2026-01 (MANDATORY STANDARD)

A simple set of rules that turns AI safety into four real checkpoints: Declare (lock the plan before you start), Authorize (let an outside guard check the rules), Record (write down steps in advance), and Triage (test work in a locked room where the AI cannot grade itself). Includes templates for company handbooks and vendor contracts.
